# PRODUCT.md — finalwhite.lol

## Identity & Purpose
Personal homepage and digital garden of **Alba (黍秋)** at `finalwhite.lol`.

## Audience
Friends, quantitative research interviewers, academic collaborators, and readers browsing essays.

## Core Surfaces
- **Home (`/`)**: Personal introduction, featured working papers, featured projects, recent thoughts.
- **Projects (`/projects`)**: Quantitative research systems, macro event studies, and software work.
- **Papers (`/papers`)**: Working papers with expandable abstracts, BibTeX copy, and external links.
- **Thoughts (`/thoughts`, `/thoughts/[slug]`)**: Bilingual long-form essays and reading notes with optional TOC and marginalia, plus legacy `/posts/[slug]` redirects.
- **About (`/about`)**: Narrative introduction, research interests, desensitized background, skills, and channels.

## Privacy & Content Invariants
- Never publish legal real name, personal phone numbers, GPA/class rank, test scores, scholarship names, partner organization names, or internship availability logistics.
- All personal metadata, projects, papers, and thoughts live in YAML (`src/data/*.yaml`) and Markdown (`src/content/thoughts/*.md`) so content updates never require editing layout code or rebuilding visual structure.
- Poetic signature `歌未竟，东方白` stays preserved in a restrained typographic register.
